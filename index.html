Listing Studio Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Go Play Emporium Listing Studio is a private tool operated by Go Play Emporium to prepare keychain listings and submit them to eBay after the seller’s explicit approval. This public page explains its data handling; it does not provide access to the private Studio.

Information handled

Studio stores seller-created listing drafts, original photographs, descriptions, item specifics, prices, shipping and return preferences, and other listing settings. It uses the signed-in Studio user’s identity to restrict access to their workspace.

When the seller connects eBay, Studio obtains and stores the seller’s eBay identifiers and authorization tokens. For listing preparation and submission it may retain eBay metadata, uploaded-photo URLs, prepared listing requests, validation results, listing IDs, and submission receipts. The current integration does not retrieve buyer information or orders.

Purpose and sharing

Information is used to identify photographed keychains, suggest editable listing content and prices, validate listings, upload photos, and submit seller-approved listings. Photos may be uploaded to eBay during preparation before publication. Listing fields are sent to eBay for validation and, after explicit approval, publication. Published listings are managed through eBay Seller Hub; Studio does not automatically edit them afterward.

When AI identification or pricing is used, relevant photos and listing information are sent to the OpenAI API. eBay authorization tokens are not included in those AI requests. The app requests that API responses are not stored using the API’s store:false setting; this is not a guarantee of zero provider retention.

Hosting and storage providers process information to operate the application. eBay and OpenAI process the information sent to their services under their respective terms and privacy policies. Studio does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Access and security

Listing Studio requires authenticated access. Original drafts and photographs are not made available by this public policy page or the notification receiver. Developer secrets remain server-side, and stored eBay access and refresh tokens are encrypted. The seller signs in on eBay to authorize the connection; Studio does not collect the seller’s eBay password. Publishing requires explicit approval and can be disabled independently of account authorization.

Retention, disconnection, and account closure

Drafts, photographs, and preferences are retained for the seller’s ongoing workflow. Integration records are retained as needed for authorization, preparation, and submission tracking, including preventing duplicate submissions. Disconnecting eBay in Studio removes its stored connection and application tokens; it does not delete drafts, original photos, other integration records, or existing eBay listings. The seller can also revoke authorization through eBay’s third-party application settings.

Marketplace Account Deletion notifications are checked against eBay’s digital signature before processing. For a matching account, Studio removes the associated production eBay connection and integration records and blocks further eBay activity to prevent stale requests from restoring the removed data. Independently created original drafts, photographs, and preferences are retained separately. Notices for accounts not held by Studio do not delete workspace data.

Only a hashed notification identifier, processing time, and match count are retained as a processing receipt, not the notification payload or eBay account identifiers. Provider-managed backups and infrastructure logs follow the hosting provider’s retention processes; application-level deletion does not promise immediate erasure of every provider backup. Deleted integration records must not be restored into active use.

Questions and requests

For questions or requests about Studio data, contact Go Play Emporium through its seller contact option on eBay. Access and correction of published listing information are managed through eBay Seller Hub. Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
